# Google Business Profile – Products (English)

Path: Edit profile → **Products** → **Add product**. Copy each field below.

- **Photo**: save the image at `Photo` and upload it (min 250×250)
- **Product name**: max 58 characters
- **Category**: type it once, then pick it from the list for the next products
- **Price**: single price, AUD, numbers only ("From" products use the lowest price)
- **Description**: max 1000 characters, no URLs or phone numbers (Google rejects them)
- **Button**: `Learn more` → paste the `Link`
