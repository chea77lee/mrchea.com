What is a private Sydney tour?

A private tour means your group travels alone with the guide — no strangers, no fixed coach timetable. Every trip on mrchea.com is designed and led in person by Mr Chea, a Sydney local guide. Tours normally run as small join tours, and any of them can be booked privately instead. On a private tour, start time, route, lunch, and when to slow down are all shaped around your group's ages, energy and interests.

It suits families with parents or children, honeymooners, friends who want an active day, and mixed Korean–English groups who want one guide everyone can understand.

Questions

What is the difference between a private tour and a join tour?

On a join tour you share the day with other guests on a set itinerary. On a private tour it is your group only, and start time, route, meals and breaks are planned around you.

Which languages does the guide speak?

Mr Chea guides in both English and Korean, so mixed-language groups and families can travel together without an interpreter.

What is the minimum group size?

Join tours run from 2 guests for Sydney day tours and from 4 guests for road trips and two-city trips. Private tour conditions are confirmed when we talk.

Do you pick up from my hotel?

Yes, pick-up and drop-off at Sydney city hotels is arranged when we plan the day, and the route is built around where you are staying.

How much does a private Sydney tour cost?

On a join tour, half-day Sydney tours start from AUD $210 per person and full-day tours from AUD $390 per person. Private tour pricing depends on group size and dates and is quoted when you enquire.
