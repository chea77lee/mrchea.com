How the 18-day crossing of Australia works

We meet at Sydney Airport and open the journey with the Harbour Bridge and Opera House by night. From there it's straight to the red desert of Uluru and Kata Tjuta, then Melbourne and the Twelve Apostles on the Great Ocean Road, and down to Hobart in Tasmania. Back in Sydney for a second night view, then across to the final stage, Western Australia: the Lancelin dunes, the Pinnacles Desert, pink Hutt Lagoon, Shark Bay, Wave Rock and the Valley of the Giants Tree Top Walk. Eighteen days that cross the continent from east to west twice, ending with your departure from Perth.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Arrive in Sydney, meet your guide; Harbour Bridge and Opera House by night
  2. Day 2 — Sydney to Uluru (domestic flight); Kata Tjuta
  3. Day 3 — Uluru sunrise tour and base walk
  4. Day 4 — Uluru to Melbourne (domestic flight); Melbourne city tour
  5. Day 5 — Great Ocean Road and Twelve Apostles day tour
  6. Day 6 — Free day in Melbourne
  7. Day 7 — Melbourne to Hobart, Tasmania (domestic flight); Salamanca Market
  8. Day 8 — Port Arthur and Richmond
  9. Day 9 — Freycinet National Park (Wineglass Bay)
  10. Day 10 — Hobart to Sydney (domestic flight); Sydney revisited, second night view
  11. Day 11 — Sydney to Perth (domestic flight); Kings Park
  12. Day 12 — Fremantle and Rottnest Island
  13. Day 13 — Lancelin dunes and the Pinnacles Desert
  14. Day 14 — Pink Hutt Lagoon and Kalbarri National Park
  15. Day 15 — Shark Bay
  16. Day 16 — Wave Rock and Hyden
  17. Day 17 — Fitzgerald, Albany, Valley of the Giants Tree Top Walk and Busselton
  18. Day 18 — Depart from Perth
