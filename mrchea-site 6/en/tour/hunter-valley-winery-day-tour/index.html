How the Hunter Valley winery tour works

A little over two hours out of the city, vineyards open up across the gentle hills of the Hunter Valley. We taste at two wineries with quite different characters, and on the way back come close to a mob of wild kangaroos out in the fields. A day away from city noise, with a glass tilted slowly.

  1. Hotel pickup
  2. Drive to the Hunter Valley
  3. Tasting at winery one
  4. Tasting at winery two
  5. Wild kangaroo spotting
  6. Hotel drop-off
