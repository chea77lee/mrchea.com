How the 10-day New Zealand North and South Island trip works

Starting with the North Island highlights of Auckland and Rotorua, crossing Cook Strait by ferry to the South Island, then on to the snow peaks of Queenstown, Milford Sound and the gardens of Christchurch — a complete route that properly joins New Zealand's two islands.

Itinerary at a glance

  1. Day 1 — Arrive in Sydney; orientation
  2. Day 2 — To Auckland (international flight); Sky Tower
  3. Day 3 — Rotorua geothermal area and Māori cultural performance
  4. Day 4 — Hobbiton Movie Set
  5. Day 5 — Travel to Wellington; Te Papa museum
  6. Day 6 — Cook Strait ferry to Picton on the South Island; on to Queenstown
  7. Day 7 — Queenstown Skyline Gondola
  8. Day 8 — Milford Sound cruise
  9. Day 9 — Travel to Christchurch; Canterbury gardens
  10. Day 10 — Back to Sydney (international flight)

The detailed itinerary and price are confirmed after we talk, to suit your group size and season.
