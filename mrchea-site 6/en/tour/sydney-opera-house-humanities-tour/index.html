How the Opera House and city tour works

The guided tour inside the Opera House is already included in the price, so you walk straight in with nothing to book. The green of the Royal Botanic Garden, the red brick of The Rocks, the Victorian stonework of Town Hall and the QVB — over one day we read the city slowly, from the first page to the last. I'll recommend a lunch spot, a café along the way and a bar for the evening to match your taste.
