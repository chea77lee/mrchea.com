How the Sydney swimming tour works

The wave-washed rock pool at Bondi Icebergs, North Sydney Olympic Pool with the Harbour Bridge straight ahead, or a hidden pool close to your hotel — we pick the day's swim spot to suit your taste and where you're staying. Swim, dry off, watch the colour of the water for a while. That's a full day's worth.

Choose your spot

  1. Option A — Bondi Icebergs ocean rock pool
  2. Option B — North Sydney Olympic Pool (Harbour Bridge view)
  3. Option C — a recommended pool near your hotel

One spot per tour, confirmed after we've talked it through.
